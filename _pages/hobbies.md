#### Books

I deeply enjoy reading fantasy and science fiction books. I've developed a taste for epic fantasy---the bigger the books the better! This taste marries well with my favorite author, <a href='https://www.brandonsanderson.com/' target=_blank>Brandon Sanderson</a>, as he seems to enjoy writing at the speed of light. I am always looking out for every new book released by BrandoSando, with a strong preference for <a href='https://coppermind.net/wiki/Cosmere' target=_blank>Cosmere</a> content.

Right now I'm diving in the <a href='https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Erikson' target=_blank>Steven Erikson</a>'s <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/series/43493-malazan-book-of-the-fallen' target=_blank>_Malazan Book of the Fallen_</a> tale.

#### Movies

Although any movie from any category has a chance to floor me with an amazing experience, I do have a preference towards fantasy, science fiction, and horror movies. The horror movies I enjoy the most tend to come frequently from Spain and Argentina. If I was put on the spot to mention only a single horror movie I would have to go with <a href='https://www.imdb.com/title/tt7549892/' target=_blank>Aterrados (2017)</a>. It really did a number on me... The horror movie I've watched the most has to be <a href='https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1038988/' target=_blank>REC (2007)</a>.

If you find yourself in Portugal by September and also enjoy horror movies, please consider attending the <a href='https://www.motelx.org/' target=_blank>MOTELX</a> horror movie festival.
